# Public claim scope

This record separates implementation documentation from promises, rights
statements, and legal conclusions.

## Normative release surfaces

The current release boundary is defined by `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`,
`PATENT-NON-AGGRESSION-PLEDGE.md`, `TRADEMARKS.md`,
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, `LICENSE-MAP.md`, the claim register, and the
source-provenance record. The Apache-2.0 text and file-level notices control;
marketing, ADR, whitepaper, and field-note language cannot expand them.

## Evidence surfaces

`docs/LIVE_BASELINE.json`, generated benchmark reports, tests, ADRs, field
notes, whitepapers, and architecture guides are evidence or design records.
Any timing, throughput, cost, reliability, security, determinism, or provider
behavior statement in them must be read with its workload, host, date, and
limitations. A historical number is not a product SLA, warranty, comparative
claim, or regulatory/security certification.

Source identifiers, validation labels, fixture text, and executable test names
can preserve an internal invariant or historical vocabulary. They are not, by
themselves, a representation that the whole process, every host, or every
downstream integration has that property.

## Authorship and provenance

An author line records document stewardship; it does not decide inventorship,
copyright ownership, co-authorship, chain of title, or patentability. External
projects and organizations are not treated as co-authors or licensors without
documented permission. Copied or migrated material remains subject to its
source license until verified.

## Release rule

If a claim or attribution cannot be tied to a reproducible record or lawful
provenance, narrow it, label it as an unverified hypothesis, preserve the
original source terms, or remove it from the release artifact.
